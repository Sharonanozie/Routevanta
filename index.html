ABOUT ROUTEVANTA

Technology should make delivery simpler.

Routevanta is an early-stage logistics technology startup working toward a cloud-based platform for last-mile delivery management.

Our mission is to make intelligent delivery operations accessible to ambitious businesses—starting with practical tools for dispatch, visibility, and coordination.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is Routevanta available to use today? +

Not yet. Routevanta is at the early product development stage. The linked dashboard is an interactive simulation, not a live fleet management service.

Does Routevanta deliver parcels itself? +

Our proposed business is logistics software, not a courier fleet. We aim to help merchants and courier businesses manage their own delivery operations.

Who is Routevanta being built for? +

We're designing for online stores, courier teams, and growing businesses that coordinate multiple daily deliveries.

Will the platform include AI features? +

Data-informed routing and performance insights are on the roadmap. We plan to validate these capabilities with users before describing them as live features.
